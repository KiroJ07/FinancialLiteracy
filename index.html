Welcome!

This website is dedicated to raising awareness among youth about the importance of financial literacy. Learn how to budget, save, invest, and make smart financial decisions from an early age.

If you're in Egypt and want to learn about the stock market, investing, and money management—whether you're a student aged 14–19 or even older—there are excellent and easy courses you can start with. Many of them are even free or available at a very low cost.

The power of Compound Interest:

Compound interest is the interest you earn on interest. This can be illustrated by using basic math: if you have $100 and it earns 5% interest each year, you'll have $105 at the end of the first year. At the end of the second year, you'll have $110.25.



Egyptian online courses that are free or nearly free:

🟢 1. Thndr Learn

https://learn.thndr.app


📌 This platform is part of the Thndr trading app.
They offer Arabic courses for complete beginners.
Here’s what you’ll learn:
• What is a stock? A stock market? Dividends?
• How to start investing with just 100 EGP?
• Concepts like risk and diversification

✅ Perfect for your age
✅ Simple explanations in Arabic
✅ Short videos with Egyptian examples
✅ You can even start with a demo account


🟢 2. Central Bank of Egypt Initiative – “Start Right”

https://lms.ebi.gov.eg/enrol/index.php?id=7435

This is an educational initiative that provides simplified content about:
• Saving
• Dealing with banks
• Investing and financial basics
• How to start saving and investing
• Information about bank cards, loans, and saving plans
• How to manage your money and plan your budget

✅ In Arabic
✅ You can find these courses in youth centers or online
✅ Extremely helpful for building a solid financial mindset
✅ Designed for teens and young adults
✅ Easy to access online
✅ Backed by a trusted official institution


🟢 3. Edraak

https://www.edraak.org

Edraak is a platform that provides free Arabic Courses.
📘 Courses that are perfect for your age, such as:
• Money management skills
• How to start investing
• Managing your allowance

✅ Short and simple videos
✅ Interactive with exercises and quizzes
✅ Certificates available upon completion


:🌍 Free Global Courses That Are Super Useful:

🔵 4. Khan Academy – Personal Finance

https://www.khanacademy.org/college-careers-more/personal-finance


In English, but very simple and beginner-friendly.
You’ll learn:
• What are taxes?
• How to choose the right investment for you
• How to avoid debt


🔵 5. Coursera – Financial Planning for Young Adults

https://www.coursera.org/learn/financial-planning


A course offered by the University of Illinois
💬 What you’ll learn:
• How to plan your finances early
• What a credit score means
• Saving vs. investing

🎓 You can take it for free by choosing “Audit” or applying for Financial Aid

Quick Note: You can also take free programming courses the same way — from major companies like Meta and Google, with internationally recognized certificates on Coursera.
